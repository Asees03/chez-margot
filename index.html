About Chez Margot

Our Story

At Chez Margot, our story begins with a love for the simple pleasures of French life — the aroma of freshly baked croissants, the warmth of a perfectly brewed coffee, and the joy of sharing a delicious meal. Inspired by the charming cafés of Paris and the timeless traditions of French cuisine, we created a place where every visit feels like a little escape to France. We believe that great food is more than just a meal; it's an experience filled with warmth, flavour, and beautiful memories.


Our kitchen brings together classic French flavours, fresh ingredients, and a passion for creating dishes that delight the senses. From comforting bistro favourites to delicate pastries, every plate is prepared with care and attention to detail. Whether you're joining us for a relaxed brunch, a romantic evening, or a sweet treat with friends, Chez Margot welcomes you to slow down, savour the moment, and discover the magic of French dining — one bite at a time.
